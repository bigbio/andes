# Heritage

andes began as a fork of MS-GF+ (Kim & Pevzner, *Nat Commun* 2014;5:5277) and
was independently rewritten in Rust. Its generating-function scoring was
replaced with an empirical rank/intensity model, and its bundled scoring models
are trained on public data — so the current engine and models share no code or
model data with MS-GF+, which remains the cited origin and inspiration.
